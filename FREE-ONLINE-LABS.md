# Free Online Labs — Hosted, No Download, No Payment

Hands-on hacking labs that run **online** (in the browser, or over VPN/SSH to the
platform's own servers) and are **free** — no VM to download, no card required.
Everything here needs at most a free account.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Authorized practice only.** These are sanctioned training platforms. Attack
> only the targets they provide; never point tooling at anything else.

> [!NOTE]
> This list is **online-hosted only** — for free labs you run on your own machine
> (GOAD, Metasploitable, VulnHub), see
> [`FREE-AD-SMB-Practice.md`](./FREE-AD-SMB-Practice.md). Free tiers change; verify
> on each site.

**Access legend:** 🌐 browser-only · 🔑 free account · 🛡️ free VPN · 💻 SSH

## Where to start (100% free, online)

1. **PortSwigger Web Security Academy** — web fundamentals, no account even needed.
2. **TryHackMe** free rooms + **OverTheWire Bandit** — Linux & tooling basics.
3. **picoGym / Root-Me** — broaden across CTF categories.
4. **Hack The Box** Starting Point + active machines — full boxes.
5. **CyberDefenders / LetsDefend** — try the defensive side.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Authorized, platform-provided targets only.
